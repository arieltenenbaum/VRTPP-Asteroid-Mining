## What This Session Investigated

### Are we getting better results than the paper?

The short answer is: **we don't know yet, because a warm-start bug was preventing the NLP from finding the true cost of FG3-based routes.**

**Key finding — FG3→Bennu NLP local minimum problem:**

Our NLP was computing FG3→Bennu at **11.43 km/s** while the paper finds **7.32 km/s** for the same arc. Both are valid local minima of the Lambert delta-v landscape — the NLP was just starting in the wrong basin.

| Arc | Paper | Our pre-fix run | Difference |
|-----|-------|-----------------|-----------|
| Earth → FG3 | 9.51 km/s | 10.19 km/s | ~0.7 km/s |
| **FG3 → Bennu** | **7.32 km/s** at T_t=7.06 TU | **11.43 km/s** at T_t=3.45 TU | **4.1 km/s** |
| Bennu → Earth | 8.17 km/s | ~9.9 km/s (init only) | ~1.7 km/s |

With FG3→Bennu costing 11.43 km/s instead of 7.32 km/s, the MILP correctly concluded FG3 routes were too expensive and explored other options (1989 ML, Anteros). Our 2-spacecraft solution with objective ≈ 18.69 was the best it could find given those inflated FG3 costs — but it was never comparing fairly against the paper's route.

**Why the warm-start bug happened:**

`initialize_mass_ratios` does a grid scan over (T_d, T_t) ∈ [0,13] × [1,13] and finds good (T_d, T_t) values — including the low-dv basin for FG3→Bennu near T_d=9, T_t=7. But it only returned the mass ratio scalar; the (T_d, T_t) values were discarded.

In iteration 1, `optimize_segment` had no warm-start so it defaulted to `T_d_min + Hohmann transfer time` (T_d≈4.47, T_t≈3.57 for FG3→Bennu). The NLP started in the wrong basin and found the 11.43 km/s local minimum. Being a gradient-based local solver (trust-constr), it never escaped.

## Bug 13 Fixed (this session) — commit `ee44c4b`

**Cell 21** | `initialize_mass_ratios`  
**Cell 24** | `solve_vrtpp_pr`

`initialize_mass_ratios` now also returns `init_times: Dict[(i,j) -> (T_d, T_t)]` — the best departure and transfer times found during the grid scan + L-BFGS-B refinement. The L-BFGS-B section was also fixed to update `best_td, best_tt` from `res.x` when it improves (previously it only updated `best_dv`, discarding the refined times).

`solve_vrtpp_pr` now pre-populates `arc_results` from `init_times` before iteration 1:
```python
arc_results = {arc: {'T_d': t[0], 'T_t': t[1]} for arc, t in init_times.items()}
```
This means the first NLP call for each arc starts from the grid-scan optimum instead of T_d_min.
